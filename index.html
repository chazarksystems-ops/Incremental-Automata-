            How to play Incremental Automata

            Everything on the board is real Conway's Game of Life. Nothing is animated or faked: the machines work only because of the Life rule (B3/S23).

            What you see

            • Green cells: live cells. A Gosper glider gun (the big shape top-left) makes a new glider every 30 generations.
            • Dashed line from a gun: where its gliders will fly (its stream). Orange means nobody collects that stream yet; green means a collector is on it.
            • Orange gate + strip: a collector. Each glider that crosses the gate in the arrow's direction pays 1 ⚡.
            • Dotted box around a gun: its insulating border. Guns need 2 empty cells between them, or they wreck each other.
            • Red: trouble. A red line or ✕ means a stream will hit something, and red circles at the edge are gliders lost uncollected.

            How to play

            1. Follow the Goal card at the top right. It tells you exactly what to click, and highlights it.
            2. Earn energy (⚡) by collecting gliders, then spend it in the Shop.
            3. Each gun needs its own collector. Use Add collector next to a new gun under Machines.
            4. Unsure about a change? Start experiment, make the change, press Test, then keep or discard it.

            Controls

            Space play/pause · N step · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo/redo · C move collector · R rotate · M mirror · Esc cancel · F fit · wheel zoom · drag empty space to pan · Shift = don't snap

            The Playtest checklist (right panel, under Production; click it to open) lists what we'd like you to try. It ticks itself. When you're done, press Show playtest report and send us the text. Nothing is uploaded automatically.
